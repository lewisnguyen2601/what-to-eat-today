# 🍜 What to eat today? 

Can't decide what to eat? Open a case and let the reel decide.

**What to eat today?** is a food and drink randomizer that works like a mystery case. Pick a meal, open the case, and watch dishes and drinks roll past until the reel slows down and stops on your meal. Then order it in one tap or find it on Google Maps.

🔗 **Live site:** https://lewisnguyen2601.github.io/what-to-eat-today/

---

## ✨ Features

- **Case-opening spins** for five meals a day: breakfast, lunch, afternoon snack, dinner and late night. Cards roll past in random order, slow down and stop on your result, with sound effects and a Skip button.
- **Food + drink combos that go together**: the drink is always chosen to suit the dish (pho with iced tea, steak with red wine, cake with a latte…).
- **Budget per meal** with a slider or a typed amount, or one budget for every meal. Results land within ±30% of the budget, based on each item's usual price.
- **Luxury spin**: only premium dishes, drinks and high-end restaurants in your city, including Michelin-starred restaurants.
- **12 countries** with local menus: Vietnam, Canada, the US, the UK, France, Italy, Germany, Australia, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan and Singapore, plus a world menu for anywhere else.
- **Local prices in local currency** (₫, $, CA$, £, €, A$, ¥, ₩, NT$, S$), shown as usual price ranges.
- **Use my location**: fills in your country, state or province, and city (the position is matched in the browser and never sent anywhere).
- **One-tap ordering**: ShopeeFood and GrabFood in Vietnam; Uber Eats, DoorDash, SkipTheDishes, Deliveroo, Just Eat, Wolt, Baemin, foodpanda and more elsewhere, each in its brand colour.
- **See photos** on Google Images and **find nearby** places on Google Maps.
- **Smart daily rules**: no two meals of the same kind in a day, snacks only in the afternoon and late at night, no caffeine late at night, and age-appropriate drinks.
- **Brand favourites** such as Starbucks (full menu), McDonald's, KFC, Highlands Coffee, Phúc Long, Gong Cha and Tim Hortons.
- **English and Vietnamese**, light and dark mode, and a mobile-friendly layout.

## 🛠️ Built with

Plain **HTML, CSS and JavaScript** in a single file, with no frameworks, no build step and no backend. Sounds are generated with the Web Audio API, and settings are saved in the browser with `localStorage`.

- Prices are estimated usual ranges for each country and may differ from place to place. Please check the delivery app or the restaurant before choosing your meal.
